# Tax Character Proof

## Rule

**The distribution happened. Its character may still be unknown.**

## Workflow

1. Record the cash distribution independently from tax character. Record a declared distribution as declared, with its scheduled pay date, never as received. Mark it received only from observed cash or reinvestment evidence, on the date that evidence shows. Take the tax-year date from the character source, which can differ from both.
2. Grade every character source. Treat interim issuer estimates as estimates unless the governing source says otherwise.
3. Apply basis-changing treatment such as return of capital only when the character evidence meets the required authority for that workflow.
4. If character is estimated, show the expected effect as provisional and keep final accounting open.
5. When final authoritative documents arrive, supersede the provisional split and recompute dependent basis/tax views deterministically.
6. Keep this workflow informational/accounting-focused; do not turn uncertain character into personalized tax advice.

## Falsify it

Try to separate the observed payment from the final tax characterization. If only an estimate or preliminary notice exists, keep tax character provisional.

## Stop conditions

- Tax character is unknown but a basis-changing write is requested.
- The source hierarchy is not established for the jurisdiction/workflow.

A stop is an output, not an excuse. State what evidence or authority would unblock the work.

## Output

DISTRIBUTION OBSERVED · CHARACTER BREAKDOWN · EVIDENCE GRADES · BASIS EFFECT · FINAL/PROVISIONAL STATUS · FOLLOW-UP SOURCE.

Keep the report short enough to act on. Preserve hard facts, unknowns, and blockers.
