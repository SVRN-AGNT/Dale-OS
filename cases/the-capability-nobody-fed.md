# The Capability Nobody Fed

## Failure
A scoring pipeline judged items of one kind by the rules for another kind. The investigation found a formula that reproduced the bad numbers exactly and concluded the system could not tell the kinds apart. It could. A router for the kinds existed and was used elsewhere, but one entry path never called the classifier, so the router fell back to a weak guess. The investigator's own output showed a count of correctly handled items far below the population, and nobody asked why.

## Root mechanism
A correct component starved of its input, misdiagnosed as a missing component because the numbers reconciled.

## Rule paid for
Before declaring a capability missing, search for it. A count that does not match its population, and two paths that should agree but do not, are leads to chase, not details to narrate.
