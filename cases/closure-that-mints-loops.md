# Closure That Mints Loops

## Failure
A work queue held roughly two open items for every closed one, and two sibling repositories had never checked off a single item. Each "closure audit" produced a list of new follow-up tracks as its product. Items blocked on a human gate kept spawning adjacent polish as new open work.

## Root mechanism
Capture had four push mechanisms and closure had one pull mechanism. Blocked closures converted one stuck item into many new ones, and completed items stayed in capped tiers, silently shrinking the caps.

## Rule paid for
Retire what you close, enter capped tiers by displacement, escalate the gate instead of multiplying its children, and report net loops.
